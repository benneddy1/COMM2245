About Me

Hello, my name is Benneddy Willie. I am currently a senior as of December 2025 studying finance at the University of New Mexico. I am native American and I grew up here in New Mexico in Gallup. I did have a little jump start into college because in my junior and senior year of high school, I attended a program in Gallup called McKinley Academy where it accelerates you into college as a high school student. There are currently different branches of the University of New Mexico in areas of the state with Gallup being one of them. As I arrived for my first time here at the main campus in Albuquerque, I believed I have already had close to enough credits to become a junior level college student. During my time at Mckinley Academy, I had an interest in learning to do video editing just because I was amazed at some creations people have shared on online platforms like YouTube and TikTok. That changed because those YouTube videos had shown videos of people reselling shoes during that time and that was the trend at the time because you could profit your money 3 times or more because of how popular some shoes were. I thought of it as an investment similar to stocks even though they are very risky and volatile. That led me to go into finance to learn more about how companies operate and invest. I think as of right now, my career that I would be most interested in is an investment fund manager planning and directing strategies for institutions.
