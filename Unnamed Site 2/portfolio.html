For the portfolio, here is a picture from the PowerPoint slides we did. Here was the executive summary slide showing a recommendation for a sell recommendation providing evidence that the stock is heavily overvalued. We reviewed a software company called Palantir that specializes in AI technology that organizes your data. Palantir is in the S&P 500 (top 500 companies in the United States) and as of December 1st, 2025, the market price was 167.49 dollars per share. What our group has done is forecast future cash flows using 3% sales growth which is from the gross domestic product growth, using 27.38% as the operating margin which is the industry average in Software- System & Applications (SSAS), and using 1.71 as the Sales-Capital ratio which is also from the industry average. After gathering other information we then found the true value which is called the intrinsic value using the discounted cash flow model to find that the value of Palantir is 90.35 dollars per share meaning that the market value is heavily overvalued is -92% mispricing.

But what would make it a buy?

Some scenarios that would allow Palantir to become a buy would be to assume a higher long-term growth rate, higher long-term operating margin, and a lower average cost of capital. In order for this to happen, Palantir’s software AI would have to become the industry average, lower perceived risk from the investors, and adjust their reinvestment cost became it is a lot of money to support/grow their AI. This is unlikely, but you can’t predict the future. Although we did mention a sell for short-term, long-term wise I would consider a buy.
